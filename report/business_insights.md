# Business Insights

## 1. Catalogue Composition

Netflix contains approximately **8.8K titles**, including **6.13K Movies** and **2.68K TV Shows**. Movies represent approximately **69.6%** of the catalogue, while TV Shows represent approximately **30.4%**.

**Insight:**  
The catalogue is heavily weighted toward movie content, indicating a larger representation of movies compared with television series.

---

## 2. Geographic Content Distribution

The **United States, India, and the United Kingdom** are the top three countries contributing content to the dataset.

**Insight:**  
Netflix's content catalogue is geographically diverse but concentrated among several major content-producing markets.

---

## 3. Genre Distribution

The most common genres include **Drama, International Movies, and Comedies**.

**Insight:**  
The catalogue has a strong presence of drama, international content, and comedy, combining mainstream entertainment with internationally produced programming.

---

## 4. Content Rating Distribution

The three most common ratings are **TV-MA, TV-14, and R**.

**Insight:**  
A substantial portion of the catalogue is targeted toward mature and teenage audiences rather than exclusively child-oriented audiences.

---

## 5. Catalogue Growth

The release-year analysis shows how Netflix's catalogue composition has changed over time, with a substantial increase in titles during the more recent years represented in the dataset.

**Insight:**  
Netflix's catalogue expanded significantly over time, demonstrating the importance of analyzing content growth alongside genre, geography, and audience characteristics.

---

## Key Takeaway

The analysis shows that Netflix has a large and diverse catalogue, but its content is concentrated around **Movies, major international markets, popular genres such as Drama and Comedy, and mature/teen ratings**.

These findings provide a high-level view of catalogue composition but do not measure audience preferences or content performance.
