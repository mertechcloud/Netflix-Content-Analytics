# Business Recommendations

Based on the SQL analysis and Power BI dashboard, the following recommendations can be considered.

## 1. Monitor Movie and TV Content Balance

Movies represent approximately **69.6%** of the catalogue.

Netflix could continue monitoring the balance between Movies and TV Shows when planning future content expansion.

> Catalogue volume alone does not indicate whether one content type performs better, so viewing and engagement data would be needed before recommending a major shift.

---

## 2. Maintain International Content Diversity

The United States, India, and the United Kingdom are among the largest contributors to the catalogue, while International Movies are also among the most common genres.

Netflix could continue evaluating geographically diverse content to maintain a broad international catalogue.

---

## 3. Monitor High-Volume Genres

Drama, International Movies, and Comedies are among the most common genres.

Netflix could compare the availability of these genres with viewing hours, completion rates, and subscriber engagement to determine whether high catalogue volume is associated with strong audience demand.

---

## 4. Analyze Audience Segments

TV-MA, TV-14, and R are among the most common ratings.

Netflix could combine catalogue data with audience engagement data to determine whether the current content mix aligns with the preferences of different audience segments.

---

## 5. Combine Catalogue Data with Performance Data

The current analysis describes **what Netflix offers**, but it does not show **what audiences watch or what content performs best**.

A future analysis could combine catalogue data with:

- Viewing hours
- Completion rates
- Subscriber engagement
- Regional performance
- Content production costs
- Revenue or retention metrics

This would allow Netflix to make more evidence-based content investment decisions.
