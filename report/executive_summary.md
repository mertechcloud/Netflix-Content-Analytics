# Executive Summary

## Netflix Content Analytics

This project analyzes Netflix's content catalogue using **Python, SQL, SQLite, and Power BI** to identify patterns in content type, geography, genres, ratings, directors, and catalogue growth.

The dataset contains approximately **8.8K titles**, consisting of **6.13K Movies** and **2.68K TV Shows**. Movies account for approximately **69.6%** of the catalogue.

The **United States, India, and United Kingdom** are the largest content-contributing countries. The most common genres include **Drama, International Movies, and Comedies**, while **TV-MA, TV-14, and R** are among the most common content ratings.

The Power BI dashboard presents these findings through interactive KPI cards, trend analysis, category charts, geographic visualization, and slicers.

### Key Business Takeaway

Netflix has a large and geographically diverse catalogue, but the catalogue is concentrated toward movie content, several major content-producing markets, popular genres, and mature/teen ratings.

### Analytical Limitation

This project analyzes **catalogue composition rather than audience behavior**. The dataset does not provide viewing hours, engagement, revenue, or subscriber information. Therefore, recommendations about content performance or future investment should be validated using additional business data.
