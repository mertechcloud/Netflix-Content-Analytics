# Netflix Content Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Netflix content catalogue using **Python, SQL, SQLite, and Power BI**.

The goal is to transform raw Netflix catalogue data into actionable business insights by performing data cleaning, exploratory analysis, SQL business analysis, and interactive dashboard development.

---

## Business Questions

The analysis explores:

- How is Netflix's catalogue divided between Movies and TV Shows?
- Which countries contribute the most content?
- Which genres are most common?
- What are the most common content ratings?
- Which directors contribute the most titles?
- How has Netflix's catalogue changed over time?

---

## Key Findings

- **8.8K titles** were analyzed.
- **6.13K Movies** and **2.68K TV Shows** are included in the dataset.
- Movies represent approximately **69.6%** of the catalogue.
- The **United States, India, and United Kingdom** are the top content-contributing countries.
- **Drama, International Movies, and Comedies** are among the most common genres.
- **TV-MA, TV-14, and R** are among the most common ratings.

> Catalogue volume does not necessarily indicate audience preference or content performance. Additional viewing, engagement, and revenue data would be required for those conclusions.
